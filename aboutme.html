The Ego

Chase Kuhn (b. 2004) is a photography student at UNC Greensboro - born in Orlando Florida before moving up to 

North Carolina in 2006 to spend a very fulfilling childhood exploring, creating and destroying.


As a creative Chase finds interest in using art as a vessel to express and explore oneself, branching out into many 

different mediums and practices due to what can only be called necessity of being – never placating in one or another 

mode of aesthetic operation and using art as way to feel through the world, leaving glimpses of the self behind to then 

be curated and reflected upon. Mixed media is a bit of a requisite before something becomes a final piece or collection, 

and while photography can often play a dominant role in this practice, it isn’t always the means to an end – 

something may fold in on itself many times over before taking its final shape, a result that is usually 

achieved by boredom rather than a sense of completion. To be complete is to die.
